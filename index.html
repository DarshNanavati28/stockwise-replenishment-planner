Formulas & assumptions

What the recommendation uses

ROP = μd × μL + z × √(μLσd² + μd²σL²); safety stock is the second term. z comes from the selected service level. Forecast is recent 7-day mean; flagged one-off events are winsorized to historical 90th percentile, while data errors are excluded. Assumes independent daily demand and lead time, approximately normal lead-time demand, stable demand during lead time, and no minimum order/pack constraints. EOQ = √(2DS/H); H = unit cost × annual holding rate. Estimated annual cost includes order, cycle-stock, and safety-stock carrying costs. Risk at ROP is approximated as 1 − service level.
